## Executive Summary
This project detects and scans vehicles on a specific road in Thailand, before verifying and calculating the preference of drivers around that area during the time it was detected. This is in analyzing the preference of what vehicle during what time of the day. For example, people might prefer to be on trucks or cars at night, while in the morning and evening there may be more motorcycles. This is to also observe vehicle quantities during times of the day, to see when the roads are the most active.
